About

I am a researcher in combinatorial optimization and operations research. My recent work addresses optimal route planning for orienteering using branch-and-cut algorithms over terrain cost surfaces with fatigue modeling, presented in an article submitted to the European Journal of Operational Research. I develop the accompanying optimization software in C++ with the HiGHS solver. I also work on Monte Carlo simulation in Python applied to quantitative finance, in particular market making.

Research interests

  • Combinatorial optimization
  • Mixed-integer programming and branch-and-cut
  • Operations research
  • Monte Carlo methods
  • Quantitative finance and market making
